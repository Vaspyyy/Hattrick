Replays that prove the level-design changes (Level design thread): every new level reaches its flag,
and every new moon coin can be picked up and brought home. Each file reaches the flag.

Replay with: ./sim LEVEL tas/proofs/FILE.tas   (add "trace" to see moons=... bits per frame)
LEVEL is the campaign index without assets/worlds.txt (worlds levels shift the numbers after them):
  1 tube town   2 brickworks   4 ramp rally   5 spikes   7 crumble canyon   8 sky   10 ember fort   11 hatrick
To check with worlds.txt in place, move it aside first, or count the levels in ./sim --check order.

New levels (each with one gimmick and three moon coins):
  tube town       tubes as hurdles, stepping stones and doors; snappers and spitters; a sideways tube through a wall
  ramp rally      slopes: crouch to roll, carry the speed over gaps; a half-pipe cave
  crumble canyon  crumble bridges, stairs and a crumble floor under a spike ceiling; springs; a tube hanging from above
  ember fort      fire bars from slow and short to long, fast and paired; a fire-bar vault
New moon coins in the older levels:
  brickworks  above the hidden block at the start; on top of the wall-jump shaft by the brick tower; in the brick cellar
  spikes      over the spike pit under the spike bar (a low long jump); above the tall tower; in the sky shortcut room
  sky         on the hidden block over the crumble walk; high over the first gap (cap jump); in the cloud garden
  hatrick     above the top of the goal stairs (backflip); on a hidden block before the stairs; in the ember vault
